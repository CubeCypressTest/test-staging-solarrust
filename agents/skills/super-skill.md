---
title: "Top Products Analysis"
description: "Use when the user asks about best-selling products, top products by revenue or quantity, or product performance rankings."
---

Produce a top products analysis:

1. List the top 10 products ranked by total revenue for the requested period.
2. For each product show: product name, category, units sold, total revenue, and share of total revenue (%).
3. Identify any product that accounts for more than 20% of total revenue and flag it as a key revenue driver.
4. Compare the current period rankings to the previous equivalent period and note any products that moved up or down significantly (3+ positions).
5. If the user specifies a category, product name, or time range, filter accordingly; otherwise default to the last 30 days across all categories.
