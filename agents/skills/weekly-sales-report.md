---
title: "Weekly Sales Report"
description: "Use when the user asks for a weekly sales summary, weekly revenue report, or week-over-week sales trends."
---

Produce a weekly sales report:

1. Report total orders and total revenue for the requested week, alongside the prior week and the week-over-week percentage change for both metrics.
2. Break revenue down by product category, sorted from highest to lowest.
3. Highlight any category whose revenue changed by more than 10% week over week.
4. Show the top 5 customers by revenue for the week.
5. If the user names a specific week or date range, scope the report to that period; otherwise default to the most recently completed week.
